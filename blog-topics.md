# Blog topic boundaries

Each article owns a specific informational question. Existing service pages retain commercial intent; the blog index is a navigation hub, not a competing service guide.

| Page | Primary intent | Keep out of scope | Service link |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| /blog/website-redesign-brief/ | What should a business owner put in a redesign brief? | Agency comparisons, local web design landing copy, broad SEO advice | /web-design/ |
| /blog/google-business-profile-holiday-hours/ | How should an owner manage date-specific holiday opening hours? | General local SEO guides, ranking tactics, website planning | /seo/ |
| /blog/ai-search-crawlers-vs-training-bots/ | How do search, training, and user-requested AI visits differ? | Broad AI optimization guides, content strategy, ranking promises | /ai-search/ |

Every article has a distinct title, description, self-referencing canonical URL, and one relevant commercial next step. Do not create duplicate category/tag archive pages for these three posts. Keep new articles focused on questions not already answered by an existing page; update an existing article when the intent is the same.

After launch, review query-to-page overlap in Search Console. Shared words are not automatically cannibalization. Investigate when multiple pages repeatedly compete for the same intended query and reader task. The topic separation is an editorial design decision, not a guarantee of rankings or traffic; no search-volume claims were used.

Platform instructions were checked against the primary sources linked in each article on September 11, 2026. Recheck those sources before later revisions.
