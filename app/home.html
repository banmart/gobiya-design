What does Gobiya do?

Gobiya is a web design and search agency in Koreatown, Los Angeles. Since 2010 our team has designed, built and optimized custom websites for more than 300 local businesses, so they show up in Google, Google Maps and AI answers from tools like ChatGPT and Perplexity.

What services does Gobiya offer?

We offer seven services: website design & development (custom sites with clear content, mobile layouts and a direct path to enquire); local seo (service pages, local business info and Google Business Profile, tuned so nearby customers find you first); ai search optimization (structuring your site so ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google’s AI Overviews can read, trust and cite your business); schema & structured data (machine-readable facts about who you are, where you are and what you do, so search engines never have to guess); site speed & core web vitals (fast on every phone and every connection, with Core Web Vitals in the green); website content & copywriting (plain-spoken pages your customers actually read and search engines can actually use); website care & support (updates, fixes and holiday hours, handled, with a designer on call when you need one).

What is AI search optimization?

AI search optimization, also called GEO or generative engine optimization, is the work of making a business easy for AI assistants to find, trust and cite. We add structured data, publish clear facts about your services and location, write answers AI can quote, and make sure AI crawlers are allowed to read your site.

What does the free site review include?

The free review checks your current site’s speed and mobile experience, your local SEO and Google Business Profile, and whether AI crawlers can read your site. You get a plain-English plan of what to fix, with no obligation to hire us.

How much does a website from Gobiya cost?

The site review is free. Our Build plan, a one-page website with essential SEO set-up, starts at $999 a month. Our Grow plan, a multi-page site with ongoing local and AI search optimization, starts at $2,499 a month. There are no long-term contracts, and annual billing saves 30%.
